# Stage 5: syllable blocks and final consonants (batchim)
hangul|가|ga|consonant + vertical vowel sit side by side|가다|gada — to go
hangul|고|go|a horizontal vowel sits underneath the consonant|고기|gogi — meat
hangul|한|han|ㅎ + ㅏ + final ㄴ: the final consonant (batchim) goes on the bottom|한국|hanguk — Korea
hangul|글|geul|ㄱ + ㅡ + final ㄹ; 한글 = Hangul|한글|hangeul — the Korean alphabet
hangul|밥|bap|final ㅂ is an unreleased "p"|밥|bap — rice / meal
hangul|옷|ot|final ㅅ sounds like an unreleased "t"|옷|ot — clothes
hangul|꽃|kkot|final ㅊ also sounds like "t"|꽃|kkot — flower
hangul|강|gang|final ㅇ = "ng"|강|gang — river
hangul|닭|dak|two final consonants (ㄺ): only one is pronounced|닭|dak — chicken
hangul|음악|eumak|a final consonant slides into a next syllable that starts with ㅇ (eu-mak)|음악|eumak — music
hangul|한국어|hangugeo|ㄱ slides over: han-gu-geo|한국어|hangugeo — Korean language
